About Me

A bit about how I got here

I'm currently pursuing my Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA), and somewhere along the way frontend development became the part of coding I actually look forward to. There's something satisfying about taking a plain idea and turning it into a page someone can click through and actually use.

I'm a fresher — I haven't worked on client projects yet — but I've spent a good amount of time building things on my own: practicing layouts, styling components, fixing bugs I created myself, and slowly getting more comfortable with React. I care about clean UI, sensible spacing, and code that's easy to read later.

Right now my focus is simple: keep building real projects, keep improving my JavaScript and React fundamentals, and get better one small project at a time.
